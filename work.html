Sheet 02 · Work

Shop to site

Blue Genie Art Industries, Austin. Custom architectural and commercial fabrication. I was project lead and fabricator.

Two jobs are the ones I ran — crew, buy, hours, and the install date. The rest show range of material and client. Photographs of the pieces are on the fabrication archive. This page is the operations record, not the gallery.

01 · 2017

Houston, TX · Fiberglass

Johnson & Johnson

Labs meeting-room shell

Exterior shell for a meeting room behind the lobby desk at J&J Labs. Pieces were shaped and finished in the shop, then hung on site around a pre-built room.

What I owned

  • Stood up the shop’s first fiberglass process: resin on the pieces, prep for seaming, and a finish that had to survive a field joint.
  • Directed a crew of five or six on material purchase, hours, and the sequence from shop to Houston.
  • Hit an occupied construction window: hang the walls, resin-seam them in place, automotive-paint the junctions at night after the other trades left, then polish.
  • Led the install. A paint crew that worked with the shop handled the night paint.

02 · Shop years

Texas · Fiberglass on steel

Historic bank

Rooftop ornament reproduction

Reproduction of an original tin rooftop decoration at the oldest bank in Texas. The machinist built the armature. The rest of the build was the shop’s to deliver.

What I owned

  • Cut the pieces on the CNC and got fiberglass onto the forms to a smooth finish.
  • Connected the skins to the steel frames.
  • Owned material purchase and man-allocation for a seven-person crew.

03 · 2016

Austin, TX · Mahogany, steel armature

The Catherine

Lobby wood screens

Mahogany screens built as walls for the lobby of a luxury high-rise. Every piece was shaped with matching panels and hung on a custom steel armature.

What I owned

  • Shaped the panels so they read as one wall, not a set of parts.
  • Hung the wood on the shop’s steel armature as a coordinated install.

04 · 2018

Austin, TX · GFRC, mosaic tile

Nessie, exterior

A swimming figure for the park outside the Thinkery. The form had to take tile and live outdoors.

  • Coordinated a tile crew that flew in from New York, then installed the piece in the park.
